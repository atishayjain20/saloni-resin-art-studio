Meet Saloni

Every piece is handmade by Saloni in her studio in Ajmer, Rajasthan.

Visit Saloni Resin Art Studio at House No. 61, Hathi Bhata, Ajmer, Rajasthan, India. Open every day, 11:00 AM–4:00 PM.

What happens next
  • Reply: Saloni normally replies within 5–6 working hours.
  • Make: Typical handmade production takes 4–5 days after design and advance confirmation.
  • Deliver: Shipping is across India: free on orders of ₹1,999 or more, otherwise ₹100 within Rajasthan and ₹150 to other states. Orders use protective packaging.

Advance amount and payment terms are confirmed with the final design and quote before production starts. For an urgent date, tell Saloni the required date; she will check feasibility and try to meet it, but delivery is not promised until she confirms.

For transit damage or replacement support, contact Saloni promptly with photos, packaging, and an unboxing video. Saloni reviews the case and confirms the replacement resolution.

Privacy details

Order details and an optional image are stored in the studio’s private Google Sheet and restricted Drive folder for handling. Customer requests and uploaded images are scheduled for deletion after 90 days. After you send a request, your name, phone, and delivery details are kept only in this browser for up to 30 days to fill your next request, and are not stored separately by the studio. A short summary of each confirmed request (items, notes and request ID) is kept only in this browser for 30 days for My orders; it has no phone number, email or address. Partner attribution and private commission records are retained for payout records. When you pay online, Razorpay processes the payment: your card, UPI or bank details go to Razorpay and the studio never sees or stores them. For a paid order the studio keeps the Razorpay order and payment IDs and the amount paid for 12 months for accounts and refunds. Google reCAPTCHA checks order requests for spam. Customer reviews (first name, optional city, rating, comment and photos) are public on the home page and are kept until removed; ask for removal through @saloniresin_art_studio or 8209238541. Request correction or deletion through @saloniresin_art_studio or 8209238541. If you tap Accept on the cookie banner, Google sets cookies to measure which Google listings lead to order requests; only the request ID, product IDs, prices and quantities are sent, never your name, phone or address.
