Shipping and returns

Every piece is handmade by Saloni in her studio in Ajmer, Rajasthan.

Start a conversation ↗ Message Saloni on WhatsApp

Shipping

Shipping across India.

  1. ChargeShipping is across India: ₹100 within Rajasthan and ₹150 to other states. Orders use protective packaging.
  2. Making timeTypical handmade production takes 4–5 days after design and advance confirmation. For an urgent date, tell Saloni the required date; she will check feasibility and try to meet it, but delivery is not promised until she confirms.
  3. PickupYou can also choose Pickup in Ajmer when you send your order request.

Ordering

How an order works.

  1. RequestSend an order request on the studio page. Saloni will contact you to confirm the design, price, and delivery. Saloni normally replies within 5–6 working hours.
  2. AdvanceAdvance amount and payment terms are confirmed with the final design and quote before production starts.

Returns and cancellation

Made for you, so no returns.

  1. ReturnsEvery piece is customised for you, so there are no returns.
  2. Transit damageFor transit damage or replacement support, contact Saloni promptly with photos, packaging, and an unboxing video. Saloni reviews the case and confirms the replacement resolution.
  3. CancellationTo cancel an order, call or message Saloni on WhatsApp. Before you pay the advance, you can also send a cancel request from My orders on the studio page, on the phone or computer you ordered from. If you cancel before making starts, your advance is refunded.
