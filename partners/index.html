How it works

A small, human referral loop.

  1. ApplyShare your work, service area, and the kind of customers you help.
  2. Get your linkAfter Saloni reviews the application, you receive one unique code and link.
  3. Share a storyCustomers receive free supported basic personalisation and send an order request.
  4. Review togetherCommission is reviewed monthly after the order is paid and delivered. Saloni approves and pays manually.

Partner Circle rules

Clear terms before you share.

  • Saloni reviews applications manually and may approve, pause, or close a partnership.
  • Eligible commission is 5% of the final product subtotal, excluding shipping, after the customer has paid and the order is delivered.
  • Saloni reviews eligible orders monthly and pays manually. There is no automatic payout.
  • Cancelled, refunded, duplicate, test, self-referred, and explicitly void orders earn no commission.
  • Customers can receive free supported basic personalisation when the design allows it; the final design, price, and delivery remain subject to Saloni's confirmation.
  • Referral codes and request-time commission snapshots are kept for the private business record. A paused or closed code stops new credit; it does not rewrite past orders.
