How it works

From your wedding to your wall.

  1. Tell SaloniSend a request or message Saloni about your varmala. Saloni normally replies within 5–6 working hours.
  2. Send your flowersIn Ajmer city, Saloni picks up your varmala from your home or venue for free. From outside Ajmer, courier the flowers to the studio in Ajmer as soon as you can after the wedding; Saloni shares the address and packing tips on WhatsApp.
  3. Confirm the designSaloni confirms the design, colours and final quote with you. Advance amount and payment terms are confirmed with the final design and quote before production starts.
  4. HandmadeTypical handmade production takes 4–5 days after design and advance confirmation. For an urgent date, tell Saloni the required date; she will check feasibility and try to meet it, but delivery is not promised until she confirms.
  5. ShippedShipping is across India: free on orders of ₹1,999 or more, otherwise ₹100 within Rajasthan and ₹150 to other states. Orders use protective packaging.
