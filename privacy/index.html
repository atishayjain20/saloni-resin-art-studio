Privacy policy

This policy explains what Saloni Resin Art Studio (saloniresinart.com) collects when you order, why, and for how long.

What we collect

What we collect and why.

  1. Order detailsYour name, phone, delivery address, the items, notes and any photo you upload, so Saloni can make and deliver your order. They are kept in the studio's private Google Sheet and a restricted Google Drive folder.
  2. PaymentsWhen you pay online, Razorpay processes the payment. Your card, UPI or bank details go to Razorpay; the studio never sees or stores them. For a paid order the studio keeps the Razorpay order and payment IDs and the amount paid for 12 months for accounts and refunds.
  3. On your deviceAfter you order, your name, phone and delivery details are kept only in your browser for up to 30 days to fill your next request, and a short order summary (no phone, email or address) for My orders.
  4. ReviewsReviews you choose to post (first name, optional city, rating, comment and photos) are public on the home page until removed.

How long

How long we keep it.

  1. Requests and photosCustomer requests and uploaded images are scheduled for deletion after 90 days.
  2. Partner recordsPartner referral and commission records are kept for payout records.

Sharing and cookies

Who else sees it.

  1. Service providersRazorpay (payments), Google (Sheets, Drive and reCAPTCHA, which checks order requests for spam) and the courier that delivers your parcel. We never sell your data.
  2. CookiesOnly if you tap Accept on the cookie banner, Google sets cookies to measure which Google listings lead to orders; only the order ID, product IDs, prices and quantities are sent, never your name, phone or address. Change it any time with Cookie settings.
