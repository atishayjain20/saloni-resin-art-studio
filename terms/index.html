Prices and payment

Prices and payment.

  1. PricesPrices are in Indian rupees and are the price you pay for the product. Shipping is added at checkout: free on orders of ₹1,999 or more, otherwise ₹100 within Rajasthan and ₹150 to other states; pickup in Ajmer is free.

  3. Order confirmationA paid catalogue order is confirmed once Razorpay reports the payment. You get an order ID you can check in My orders.

Delivery and returns

Delivery, cancellation and refunds.

  2. Cancellation and refundsCancel within 24 hours of paying, before work starts, for a full refund. Pieces are personalised, so there are no returns. Full details are on Shipping and returns.
